How it works

Your card, ready
in days — not weeks.

Step 01

Tell us about the day

Share the occasion, the names, the date and any photos or motifs you love. A short WhatsApp chat is usually all it takes.

Step 02

We design your first draft

Within 48 hours you receive a private link to your card — real content, real animation, on your phone, ready to react to.

Step 03

Refine together

Change the wording, the colours, the music, the order of sections. We revise until it feels unmistakably yours.

Step 04

Share & celebrate

You get the final link (and print files if you want them). Send it to one guest or a thousand — it opens the same everywhere.
