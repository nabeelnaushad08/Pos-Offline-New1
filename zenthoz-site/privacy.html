Privacy policy

Last updated — July 2026

Zenthoz ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through zenthoz.com and our services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly — such as your name, email address, phone number and company details when you contact us, request a quote or subscribe to updates. We also collect standard technical data automatically, including your IP address, browser type, pages visited and referral source.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide our services.
  • To prepare proposals, quotes and audits you request.
  • To send updates or marketing you have opted into — you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To understand how our website is used and improve it.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

3. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences (such as your light/dark theme choice) and to measure site performance through analytics tools. You can control cookies through your browser settings; the site remains usable with cookies disabled.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (such as hosting, analytics and email platforms), with advertising platforms when you engage us to run campaigns, and where required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. Enquiry data that does not lead to an engagement is deleted or anonymised within a reasonable period.

6. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at info@zenthoz.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Third-party links

Our site links to third-party platforms such as WhatsApp and social networks. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.
